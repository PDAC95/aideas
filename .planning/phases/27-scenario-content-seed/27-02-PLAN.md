```
## Scenario: <Title in English, plain prose>

- functional_area_slug: ventas
- slug: lead-followup-after-trade-show
- sort_order: 10
- typical_hours_per_week: 3
- pain_headline_en: "I lose half my trade-show leads because I don't follow up fast enough."

- pain_body_en: |
    After every trade show I come back with a stack of business cards and the best of intentions.
    By the time I've typed them into the CRM and drafted a first email, two weeks have gone by and
    the lead has gone cold. The leads I do follow up with often get one generic email and never hear
    from me again. My competitors that respond within 24 hours are eating my lunch.
- pain_body_es: |
    Despues de cada feria regreso con una pila de tarjetas de presentacion y las mejores intenciones.
    Para cuando las he capturado en el CRM y redactado un primer correo, ya pasaron dos semanas y el
    lead se enfrio. A los que si contacto, les mando un correo generico y nunca vuelven a saber de mi.
    Mis competidores que responden en 24 horas se estan llevando mis ventas.
- impact_label_en: "~3 hrs/week saved"
- impact_label_es: "~3 hrs/semana ahorradas"
- templates:
  - lead-followup-email
  - crm-data-sync
  - pipeline-alerts
```

Field rules:
- `functional_area_slug`: MUST be one of the 8 fixed area slugs.
- `slug`: kebab-case English, globally unique across all 50 scenarios, <=80 chars, descriptive of the pain not the solution (e.g. `lead-followup-after-trade-show`, NOT `email-automation-tool`).
- `sort_order`: integer per area starting at 10, incrementing by 10 (10, 20, 30...). Independent per area — each area starts at 10.
- `typical_hours_per_week`: whole integer 1-20 inclusive. NEVER 0, NEVER decimal, NEVER >20.
- `pain_headline_*`: <=200 chars, customer first-person voice, leads with the pain, NOT the solution.
- `pain_body_*`: 3-5 sentences, customer first-person voice, includes context + concrete task example + who it affects.
- `impact_label_en`: exactly the string `"~X hrs/week saved"` where X = typical_hours_per_week.
- `impact_label_es`: exactly the string `"~X hrs/semana ahorradas"` where X = typical_hours_per_week.
- `templates`: ordered list of 2-4 existing template slugs (display_order is the list position, 0-indexed when transformed in 27-03). Most-relevant template first.
- ES copy must MATCH EN in tone and content (same pain, same scope, same length). Bilingual SIMULTANEOUS authoring per CONTEXT.md. Not literal translation — same customer voice.
